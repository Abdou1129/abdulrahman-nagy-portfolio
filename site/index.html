About

Broadcast discipline. Social-first instincts.

I'm a digital journalist and content creator who has presented on international television, run brand accounts, and shot, edited and voiced the videos myself. I know how a story works on air, and how it has to change to hold attention on a phone.

From Kuwaiti brand campaigns and agency work, to Al Jazeera Mubasher's “Know China” series filmed across eight Chinese cities, to cultural institutions in Germany such as Der Divan Berlin and Cologne's mosque communities, my job has been the same: find the human angle, film it well, and get it seen. I'm also co-founder of the creative studio Creativia.
